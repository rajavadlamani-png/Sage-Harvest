Puja Voice Lab

This is an isolated prototype. The live Sage Harvest Puja assistant is not changed by this page.

What we are testing

1) SraVaani for speech input and continuous recognition. 2) DhVaani for a natural female Puja voice. 3) Later, we will connect the successful voice components to Sage Harvest knowledge retrieval and the Gemini text model.

No software is installed on your laptop. These experiments run in the browser using hosted Hugging Face Spaces.

1. SraVaani · Live ASR

Speak naturally and see whether the streaming transcription captures complete questions without cutting you off.

2. DhVaani · Female Voice Test

Use the official Vaani TTS demo to test the available female voice options. We will choose the voice that best fits Puja.

THE PRACTITIONER BEHIND SAGE HARVEST

Deep operating experience.
Future-facing thinking.

Raja Vadlamani is a senior seed and supply-chain professional whose career has spanned nearly four decades across the seed and agri-business industry. His experience covers supply-chain leadership, seed production, processing, quality, warehousing, inventory, transformation and strategic initiatives.

His professional journey includes experience with Corteva, Advanta, Shriram Bioseed and SeedWorks International, giving Sage Harvest a perspective shaped by different organizations, operating models and stages of industry evolution.

At Sage Harvest, this experience is translated into independent advisory work—helping organizations strengthen supply chains, use data and AI more effectively, build climate resilience, evaluate transactions and pursue international opportunities.

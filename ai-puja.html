AI & Puja Notice.

Puja is an AI-assisted voice guide provided by Sage Harvest to help visitors explore information about the firm, its services and published perspectives.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

What Puja is

Puja is an AI-assisted voice interface designed to help visitors navigate and understand publicly available Sage Harvest information. Responses are generated by an AI system and may not always be complete, current or accurate.

What Puja is not

Puja is not a human adviser and its responses are not professional, legal, financial, investment, tax, regulatory or other regulated advice. Visitors should not rely on a Puja response as a substitute for advice appropriate to their circumstances.

Information submitted to Puja

Please do not provide Puja with confidential, commercially sensitive, personal, privileged or otherwise restricted information. For a specific advisory requirement, contact Sage Harvest directly so that the matter can be understood and, where appropriate, addressed through a formal engagement.

Third-party AI infrastructure

Puja currently uses a Sage Harvest-controlled Cloudflare Worker to obtain a short-lived access token and connects to Google's Gemini Live service for the voice interaction. Third-party processing is also governed by the applicable provider terms and privacy practices.

Availability

Puja is provided as an additional website feature. Availability may depend on network connectivity, browser permissions, third-party service availability and usage limits. Sage Harvest does not guarantee uninterrupted operation or error-free responses.

Contact

For a formal advisory discussion, please use the Talk to us page rather than submitting confidential information to Puja.

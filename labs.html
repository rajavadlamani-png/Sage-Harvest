SAGE HARVEST LABS

Where supply-chain experience meets data, AI and field intelligence.

A working space for research, analytical models, digital concepts and practical tools that help seed and agri-business leaders make better decisions.

01 · AI & ANALYTICS

Forecasting & Decision Intelligence

Applying statistical and machine-learning approaches to demand forecasting, supply planning and operational decision support.

  • Regression and machine-learning models
  • Demand and supply forecasting
  • Yield prediction and scenario modelling
  • Goal-oriented decision support
02 · GEOSPATIAL INTELLIGENCE

Satellite Data Intelligence

Exploring how satellite information can be acquired, processed and interpreted for field-level and supply-chain decisions.

  • Satellite data-source selection
  • Acquisition methodology and data preparation
  • Spatial and temporal interpretation
  • Vegetation and crop indicators
  • Integration with weather and contextual data
03 · CLIMATE & SUSTAINABILITY

Climate Assessment & MRV Tools

Developing practical approaches to assess climate exposure, resource efficiency, emissions and measurement-readiness across agricultural supply chains.

  • Climate assessment frameworks
  • Carbon and resource-efficiency calculations
  • MRV concepts and evidence workflows
  • Climate-informed supply-chain planning
Existing work: Seed Industry Carbon Reduction Calculator
04 · DIGITAL SUPPLY CHAIN

Data Architecture & Decision Systems

Designing the information foundations required to connect operational data, analytics and management decisions.

  • Supply-chain data architecture
  • Data flows and integration design
  • Traceability and reconciliation
  • Decision-support layers
  • Digital transformation roadmaps
05 · SEED CLIMATE INTELLIGENCE

Rice Seed Climate Ledger

A developing concept for capturing emissions-related activities and climate-reduction interventions in rice seed production.

  • Activity-level emissions mapping
  • Climate intervention tracking
  • Rice-specific operational context
  • Pathways toward evidence-based reporting
Concept: Rice Seed Climate Ledger
06 · GREENFIELD & OPERATING MODELS

From Concept to Operating System

Applying supply-chain thinking to greenfield projects, infrastructure design and operating-model development.

  • Capacity and network planning
  • Processing and storage concepts
  • Technology and equipment evaluation
  • Operating-model design
  • Implementation planning

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Independence

We provide an independent perspective based on the circumstances, evidence and objectives of each engagement.

Evidence and professional judgement

Recommendations are informed by available information, analysis, operating experience and professional judgement. Where information is incomplete or uncertain, that uncertainty should be recognised rather than concealed.

Conflicts of interest

Sage Harvest seeks to identify potential conflicts of interest before accepting an engagement and to address material conflicts appropriately.

Client responsibility

Sage Harvest provides advisory perspectives, analysis and recommendations. Responsibility for management, investment, commercial and strategic decisions remains with the relevant client, board or authorised decision-maker.

No guaranteed outcomes

Advisory recommendations involve judgement and uncertainty. Sage Harvest does not guarantee particular commercial, financial, operational, investment or other outcomes.

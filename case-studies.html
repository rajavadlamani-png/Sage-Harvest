RISK & QUALITY · ILLUSTRATIVE CASE PERSPECTIVE

US7067 GMS vs Conventional: Using Data to Identify Seed Quality Risk

An example of how lot-level quality data can move a discussion from anecdotal concern to evidence-based risk assessment.

Challenge

Assess whether quality failures were disproportionately concentrated in the GMS version of a cotton hybrid compared with its conventional counterpart.

Approach

Compare lot-level outcomes and apply statistical tests—including a Chi-square test and a two-proportion comparison—to assess whether the observed difference was likely to be meaningful rather than random variation.

Key observation

The analysis indicated a higher concentration of problematic lots in the GMS group, with an estimated relative risk of approximately 1.85× and odds ratio of approximately 2.36×. These figures should be validated against the underlying quality dataset before external use.

Management implication

Lot-level evidence can help management focus root-cause investigation, sampling and corrective action where risk is concentrated rather than treating all lots as equally exposed.

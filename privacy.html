TRUST & GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy.

This notice explains how Sage Harvest Agro Pvt. Limited handles personal information in connection with this website and its visitor-facing services.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. Who we are

Sage Harvest Agro Pvt. Limited (“Sage Harvest”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates this website and provides independent advisory services in seed and agri-business supply chains.

2. Information we may receive

We may receive information that you choose to provide when you contact us, such as your name, email address, organisation, role and the information contained in your enquiry.

When you use Puja, our AI-assisted voice guide, the service may process the text or audio you submit, together with technical information needed to establish and operate the voice session. The current website does not contain a conventional contact form or an installed analytics script.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss potential engagements, provide and operate Puja, maintain website functionality, protect the website and services, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Puja and third-party services

Puja uses a Sage Harvest-controlled Cloudflare Worker to obtain a short-lived access token and then connects to Google's Gemini Live service for the AI-assisted voice interaction. The website does not expose a Gemini API key in its client-side code.

Because Puja relies on third-party infrastructure, information transmitted through the service may also be subject to the relevant provider terms and privacy practices. Sage Harvest does not represent that third-party processing is governed solely by this policy.

5. Website hosting and technical information

The website is published using Cloudflare Pages, with source code maintained in a GitHub repository. Cloudflare may process technical information, including IP addresses and request data, to deliver, secure and operate the website under its own privacy and security practices. GitHub may process information associated with repository access and development services under its own policies.

6. Cookies and analytics

The current Sage Harvest website does not intentionally deploy its own analytics or advertising cookies. Third-party services linked from the website, including YouTube, LinkedIn or other external destinations, operate under their own privacy practices when you visit or interact with those services.

7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website or an engagement, where you have requested or authorised the disclosure, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

8. Retention

We seek to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business needs, or as required by applicable law. Specific retention periods may depend on the nature of an enquiry or engagement.

9. Your choices and requests

If you have a question about personal information held by Sage Harvest, or wish to request access, correction, deletion or other action available under applicable law, please contact us at raja.vadlamani@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Data protection framework

Sage Harvest intends to handle personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements and evolving regulatory obligations, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework as applicable to the organisation and the relevant processing activity. This notice is not a representation that every provision of that framework is currently in force or applicable to every visitor.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when our website, services or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Raja Vadlamani, Sage Harvest Agro Pvt. Limited.

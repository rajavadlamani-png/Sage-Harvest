Developer & Disclosures

Developed by Raja Vadlamani

This is an independent public scenario tool developed by Raja Vadlamani to help companies explore the potential climate and carbon implications of improved practices across seed-production landscapes.

Purpose: education, screening and scenario exploration. It is not intended to establish a company's official GHG inventory, carbon footprint, regulatory compliance position or carbon-credit entitlement.

Full disclosure: This calculator is an analytical and educational scenario tool. It is not a carbon-credit registry, certification system, validation/verification service, legal opinion, accounting opinion, investment recommendation, tax advice, or assurance statement. Results are estimates generated from user inputs and illustrative assumptions. They must not be represented as verified emission reductions, issued Carbon Credit Certificates (CCCs), offsets, removals, Scope 1/2/3 inventory results, or guaranteed financial value.
Methodology disclosure: The calculator does not substitute for an approved methodology, project design document, baseline study, monitoring plan, additionality assessment, uncertainty assessment, leakage assessment, permanence assessment where applicable, validation, verification or registry decision. The Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) currently lists BM AG04.002 — Emission reduction through improved management practices in rice cultivation under the CCTS Offset Mechanism.
Reference: BEE, “Methodologies and Tools under Offset Mechanism”, last updated 7 July 2026.
Credit disclosure: A modeled tCO₂e reduction is not automatically a carbon credit. Under the CCTS Offset Mechanism, project registration and issuance are subject to applicable eligibility, methodology, monitoring and validation/verification requirements. This tool does not predict or guarantee issuance.
Reference: BEE CCTS Offset Mechanism materials and approved methodologies.
Baseline & factor disclosure: The baseline practice share and eligible-area share are screening inputs; actual baseline, additionality, leakage, uncertainty and buffer treatment must follow the applicable methodology. This teaser applies no generic credit haircut. The crop-level reduction factors in this tool are illustrative screening placeholders for scenario analysis. They are not claimed to be official BEE/CCTS factors. For an actual project, replace them with methodology-specific equations, parameters, activity data and project/baseline calculations supported by appropriate evidence.
Ownership & claim-rights disclosure: In contract-grower seed production, the right to claim, transfer or sell reductions depends on contractual arrangements, the applicable accounting/crediting framework and programme rules. A reduction should not be counted twice across incompatible claims. This tool does not assess ownership, claim-rights or double-counting for any specific project.
GHG accounting disclosure: This calculator is not a GHG Protocol inventory calculator. The GHG Protocol Land Sector and Removals Standard provides requirements and guidance for accounting and reporting land-sector emissions and removals; its effective date is 1 January 2027. Any corporate reporting claim should be assessed against the applicable GHG Protocol requirements and the company's defined inventory boundary.
Reference: GHG Protocol, Land Sector and Removals Standard v1.1 and Guidance, 2026.
Scope disclosure: This calculator does not determine whether a company's reductions can be counted toward a corporate GHG inventory, Scope 3 category, product carbon footprint, insetting claim or any other corporate climate claim. Such treatment depends on the relevant accounting framework, boundary, ownership/control rules and claim requirements.
No guarantee: Actual carbon-reduction outcomes, credit volumes, eligibility, verification results, issuance, marketability or economic value may differ materially from this scenario output.

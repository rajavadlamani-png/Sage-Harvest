TRUST & GOVERNANCE

Confidentiality Principles.

Sage Harvest recognises that advisory work can involve commercially sensitive information, strategic decisions and operating data.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Our approach

Information shared with Sage Harvest in connection with a potential or confirmed engagement is handled with appropriate discretion and care.

  • Information is used for the purpose for which it has been provided or as otherwise agreed.
  • Access is limited, where reasonably practicable, to people or service providers who need the information for the relevant purpose.
  • Confidential information is not intentionally disclosed to third parties except where authorised, required by law, or necessary for agreed professional or service-provider purposes.
  • Appropriate safeguards are used for documents and information under our control.
  • Confidentiality expectations may continue after completion of an engagement, subject to applicable law and the terms agreed with the client.

Initial enquiries

An initial enquiry through the Sage Harvest website or by email does not, by itself, establish a formal client relationship or a comprehensive confidentiality obligation.

Please do not send commercially sensitive, confidential or restricted information in an initial enquiry. A brief description of the challenge is normally sufficient for an initial conversation.

Engagement-specific terms

Where an assignment involves material confidential information, Sage Harvest may recommend an appropriate confidentiality agreement or include confidentiality provisions in the engagement documentation. The confidentiality obligations applicable to a particular assignment will be governed by the relevant engagement letter, NDA or other written terms agreed with the client.

Puja

Puja is an AI-assisted voice guide for exploring public Sage Harvest information. It is not an appropriate channel for confidential or commercially sensitive information. Please do not submit such information to Puja.
